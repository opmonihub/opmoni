# Office ERP commercial scenario coverage plan

The [crosswalk](scenario-crosswalk.csv) enumerates the five delta capabilities in `openspec/changes/add-office-erp-commercial-management/specs/`: **26 requirements and 69 scenarios**. Each row retains its source file and line, exact capability/requirement/scenario identity, implementation task IDs, a proposed test case or explicit browser acceptance, verification type, and initial `pending` status. Proposed test paths and case names are planning references; they do not assert that files exist or tests have passed.

The references cover the distinct agreement states (draft, awaiting owner, accepted, effective, canceled), Company licence states and independent manual/automatic holds. In particular, an accepted contract awaits explicit activation before its first invoice; a previously occupied accepted sale may activate after selling rights are lost; an unaccepted invitation may not. Existing accepted grants and bounded maintenance continue after resale or office-subscription loss, while Account suspension and unrelated tenant-write gates remain enforced.

The crosswalk marks last-slot allocation, allowance revocation versus owner acceptance, concurrent period issuance, adjustment versus receipt, and competing receipts for **independent-process PostgreSQL** acceptance under task 11.1. Task 11.1 also covers the other planned races in `tasks.md`; scenario rows do not imply that sequential tests satisfy that gate. Browser rows require the stated routes and states, with task 11.4's desktop/mobile, theme, keyboard, accessibility and 320px checks where applicable. Task 11.5 provides the integrated sale-to-rights-loss flow.

All 69 statuses remain `pending`. Task 12.1 should replace planning references with actual test/browser evidence and only then update individual statuses. This document records coverage intent only; application suites, browser flows and external services were not run for this documentation task.
