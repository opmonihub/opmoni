# Office ERP commercial management — integrated acceptance checkpoint

Recorded 2026-10-25 against accepted implementation source `6c5d81bbe27e5c97113c0b23478243b9e41f209b` in the isolated `office-erp-commercial-design` worktree. This is a bounded local acceptance record, not a claim that the original 55-item plan or global acceptance is complete.

## Product boundary and routes

Office resale is an Account-scoped commercial workflow, separate from the ERP Company's own subscription, operational data and finance. The seller uses the selected office membership and office context. The seven ordinary office-admin pages are `/gestao-erp`, `/gestao-erp/planos`, `/gestao-erp/clientes`, `/gestao-erp/assinaturas`, `/gestao-erp/faturas`, `/gestao-erp/recebimentos` and `/gestao-erp/configuracoes`. The print view is `/gestao-erp/faturas/{id}/imprimir`. The legacy platform bookmark `/admin/erp/assinaturas` leads toward the office workflow but does not create office context or membership; a platform-only Super Admin must select an office where they are an actual member. The owner enters at `/erp/ativar` from an issued invitation and reads their own terms and invoices at `/erp/contrato`.

The office API lives under `/api/v1/erp-commercial`: context and summary; operation lookup; client and offer/plan/revision reads and mutations; contract lifecycle and invitation issue/revoke/reissue; invoice draft/issue/correction/cancel/print; receipts and reversals; private receipt-proof upload/download; and office commercial policy preview/read/update. Company-owner invoices are read through `/api/v1/erp/commercial-invoices`; Company subscription and settings remain separate ERP APIs. The authorization clarification under D009 is narrow: active office status, a real admin membership and selected-office assertion remain mandatory. Existing accepted contracts retain action-classified maintenance and financial-history operations after office subscription or resale-right loss. Creating/sending a contract, issuing an invitation, adopting a Company subscription, adding licences, expanding modules and creating/publishing offers remain new-sale actions requiring current commercial rights and capacity. This is not a general tenant-write bypass.

Recurring invoices are internally generated by the hourly scheduler entry `office-erp:generate-invoices --batch=50`, backed by command signature `office-erp:generate-invoices {--batch=50}` and bounded batch validation (1–100). The command dispatches eligible effective contracts with a billing cursor. It does not charge, collect, or contact a payment provider.

These office commercial invoices and receipt records are internal resale/accounting records. They do not issue NF-e, NFS-e, or another fiscal document. No payment gateway, Pix, boleto, bank confirmation, refund integration, or external payment dispatch was exercised or accepted here.

## Integrated browser evidence

The real browser flow used an ordinary office admin with a selected office and a separate authenticated owner session. It issued an owner invitation, completed the anonymous owner acceptance, created a Company, then activated the resulting contract. The flow produced the first recurring invoice for BRL 100.00. The owner could read their own commercial terms and two invoices, without seller receipt references.

The office recorded BRL 40.00 with a private PNG proof and BRL 60.00 without proof. The invoice balance progressed from BRL 100.00 to BRL 60.00 to zero. A reasoned BRL 60.00 receipt reversal reopened the outstanding balance at BRL 60.00. The browser opened the protected print route, invoked the print action, and produced a valid one-page browser PDF. Its rendered/text content showed the original BRL 100.00, received BRL 40.00 and outstanding BRL 60.00, with no fiscal issuance or payment instructions and no application-shell controls. Native operating-system print UI was not exercised.

A due BRL 0.01 invoice triggered an automatic-policy preview showing one affected contract; the operator explicitly confirmed the change. The contract/license moved to `Em atraso`. A manual hold remained `Suspensa` after the overdue amount was paid and the other BRL 60.00 remained outstanding; explicit resume returned it to `Ativa`. This demonstrates that clearing debt does not erase a manual hold.

With a private, guarded fixture disabling only the test office's resale flag, the existing contract and its unchanged-term renewal remained usable, producing revision 2 at the same BRL 100.00 terms and an active licence without Company-side effects. New-sale preview and invitation issue were unavailable while the right was disabled, and the overview showed `Novas vendas indisponíveis`. A platform-only Super Admin opening the legacy bookmark without office membership saw the office-context explanation and selection path; no seller disclosure or implicit membership was granted. Cross-office isolation for another office/client was exercised.

Other browser observations include responsive shell navigation and Escape-to-close. The B10 empty-overview contrast issue was corrected in `c898900` and rechecked in final browser pass `87228e3`: the main-scoped empty overview measured zero axe violations at 390px dark, 1366px dark and 320px light. This targeted fix does not constitute a full accessibility audit. A separate inherited moderate finding remains in the global office sidebar: its navigation landmark is unlabeled and not unique.
