## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Office Settings navigation has consistent Portuguese identity
Office Settings SHALL use consistent Brazilian Portuguese labels and ordering across its sidebar, search and persistent local navigation for equivalent authorized destinations. Related settings tasks SHALL retain the established dashboard composition, concise task guidance and supported mobile scrolling. Existing demonstration profile/settings behavior SHALL remain explicitly identified; visual consistency SHALL not invent a business save or expand access. Office/company context selection, membership enforcement and state clearing SHALL retain their existing contracts.

#### Scenario: Open the same settings destination through different surfaces
- **WHEN** an authorized office member opens a settings destination through the sidebar or local navigation
- **THEN** the destination has the same Portuguese identity and relative ordering, remains reachable at narrow widths and exposes only authorized behavior

#### Scenario: Demonstration settings remain truthful
- **WHEN** a member opens an existing demonstration profile/settings screen after navigation and copy alignment
- **THEN** its demonstration status remains visible and no real business mutation or successful persistence is fabricated
