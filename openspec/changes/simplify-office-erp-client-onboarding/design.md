## Context

See [proposal.md](proposal.md) for motivation and the five [capability deltas](specs/) for the behavior contract. This planning checkout is `/home/obsidian/dev/opmoni/.worktrees/erp-client-onboarding`, branch `codex/erp-client-onboarding`, aligned with local `main` at `babea5d`. The committed archives, canonical specifications, documentation and alert refinement are included. The earlier completed changes remain under `openspec/changes/archive/`; only this new change is active. The unrelated local `reference/sistema/` checkout is not part of the versioned baseline.

Current seams: `ErpCompanyProvisioning::createCompanyForOwner()` always establishes an owner and pending subscription; `ErpCommercialGate` accepts active subscriptions only. `OfficeErpContracts::activate()` requires owner acceptance and issues the first invoice transactionally. Contract/contract-revision offer references are already nullable, so direct assignments can reuse frozen terms without manufacturing a public offer. `ClientManager::create()` performs required CNPJ lookup before persistence. Its existing lookup failures and client limits must survive enrollment. `OfficeErpOperations` and `useOfficeErpCommand` already provide actor-bound replay and uncertain-outcome recovery. Settings read checks currently disagree between frontend and backend: the frontend requires resale-enabled `can_configure`, while policy actions are maintenance.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** Reuse Company/Client/subscription and invoice primitives; keep direct commercial assignment independent of optional owner credential setup; introduce a narrow office-attributed trial entry; make time-based entitlement correct without relying on a scheduler; produce source-bound acceptance evidence with no new dependencies.

**Non-Goals:** No replacement Company identity model, office impersonation/delegation grant, retail-offer rebuild, payment gateway, automatic email sender, live fiscal enablement or environment release switch. Do not reorganize unrelated accounting/fiscal/portfolio modules.

## Decisions

### 1. Reuse contracts for administrative and trial allocation

Choose additive administrative/trial provenance on `OfficeErpContract` rather than a second billing engine or hidden offers. Add `provisioning_source` (`legacy_invitation`, `office_admin`, `office_trial`) with default `legacy_invitation`, `erp_plan_id` for the selected technical ERP catalog entry, and `authorized_by_user_id`/`authorized_at` for attributable administrative paid terms. Existing rows keep their current revisions and status. Administrative revisions have null offer references and contain explicit canonical BRL amount, cycle, due offset and frozen allowed modules.

A trial contract is accepted for allocation attribution but not effective for billing: it has no service anchor or billing cursor and no paid invoice. The linked subscription is `trialing`. Conversion is the single transaction that writes paid terms, makes the contract effective, anchors billing and issues its first invoice. Administrative new paid enrollment performs those steps directly. Reuse `OfficeErpContracts` activation internals behind an explicit verified administrative path; never mark an office admin as the customer's consenting owner or fake owner acceptance. Standard legacy invitation activation retains its original path.

Trial allocation can reuse the existing non-null `contract_id` and one-per-contract/Company constraints. This avoids widening every allocation consumer to nullable contract IDs. `OfficeErpAdmission` counts unexpired trial allocations with paid allocations. Expiry releases only allocations whose locked subscription remains expired trial, never a converted paid allocation. An expired trial conversion must reacquire a slot; an unexpired conversion keeps its slot even after a quota downgrade, but cannot expand modules beyond current permitted new grants.

### 2. Separate provisioning and identity establishment

Extract the existing Company+establishment+pending subscription+initializer transaction into `ErpCompanyProvisioning::createUnclaimedCompany(array $companyData): ErpCompany`; retain `createCompanyForOwner(User $owner, array $companyData)` as a wrapper that adds the membership in its enclosing transaction. Track `owner_setup_status` as `unclaimed` or `claimed`; default/backfill existing Companies as claimed, and never infer that an existing Company without a loaded relationship is unclaimed.

Office direct enrollment creates an unclaimed Company when the CNPJ is genuinely new, links its own Client and sets paid terms without inventing a User. The initial ERP customer form does not collect a customer's password. Optional "Preparar acesso" uses the current revocable owner-invitation/continuation infrastructure with new `purpose=access_setup`; default `commercial_acceptance` preserves all existing tokens. `OfficeErpOwnerSetup` validates the intended name/email and verified identity, claims only an explicitly unclaimed Company under its lock, creates its owner membership, and records claim attribution. It neither activates subscriptions, allocates capacity nor creates invoices. Access setup is recoverable after office resale loss because it completes access to an already assigned subscription; Account/membership/identity security checks still apply.

For an already owned Company, return `409 company_association_required` without protected details. Its authenticated current owner can explicitly authorize association using the retained guarded confirmation mechanism. Neither office-side CNPJ matches nor existing email matches grant ownership. Commercial administration does not grant operating access; existing explicit Company delegation remains the only office operating route.

Alternative rejected: make the office administrator temporary Company owner. This would silently change ownership and delegation boundaries and confuse commercial administration with operating access.

### 3. Add office-specific trial configuration and durable trial history

Create `office_erp_trial_links` with Account FK, unique public UUID `public_code`, selected `erp_plan_id`, enabled flag, monotonically increasing revision and creator/updater attribution. One enabled configuration per Account; rotation replaces the public UUID and increases revision. The public identifier is an attribution link, not a login secret. Copy reconstructs the current-origin URL `/erp/testar/{code}`. Do not place private owner setup tokens in this link or in its operation journal.

Create `erp_subscription_trials` with subscription, Company and Account references, normalized original `cnpj`, `started_at`, `ends_at`, `converted_at`, `expired_at` and `link_revision`. Enforce global uniqueness of original normalized CNPJ and one trial per Company; retain these grants across company/portfolio archive and email changes. FK deletion is restrictive for trial provenance. The server clock supplies `started_at`; `ends_at = started_at + 168 hours`, stored in UTC. Never calculate eligibility from browser time, start of day, link issuance or a mutable email. Paid conversion retains the original trial row.

Add explicit `trialing` and `trial_expired` subscription values to PHP enum, serializers, validators and platform read filters. Add a named validity check for `erp_subscriptions` on PostgreSQL and SQLite that accepts existing legal statuses plus those two; do not alter the unrelated office `subscriptions_status_valid` constraint. Generic platform status mutation must not fabricate or reset a trial. Reuse `ErpCommercialGate` for eligibility, including a trial row whose end is strictly later than server now. Its `moduleEnabled()` must accept the linked accepted, non-effective trial contract's frozen grants only for a currently eligible trial; today that method restricts linked contracts to `effective`, so checking `allowsOperatingWrites()` alone would leave every trial module blocked. `trialing` by itself grants nothing without that valid trial row. Security/manual suspension outranks trial eligibility. Reads and Company-owner subscription pages show trial metadata to currently authorized users; seller receipt/private proof data stays private.

### 4. Reuse registration and portfolio validation without provider calls under locks

`OfficeErpTrialRegistration::register(Request $request, string $code, array $input, string $key): array` orchestrates existing identity/CNPJ validation and provisioning. The code resolves Account/plan server-side. Trial signup collects name/email, password only for a new identity, company name/CNPJ and a valid existing company tax-regime value; authenticated owners cannot submit another identity's email. Public payloads cannot select an Account or a Client ID; direct enrollment uses a scoped active Client ID or a complete new-client registration body, never both.

Extract `ClientManager::prepareCompanyRegistration(array $data): array` for validation/required external lookup and `ClientManager::createPreparedCompany(Account $account, array $prepared): Client` for locked limit checks/persistence; `create()` remains the compatible wrapper. The new `trial.register` action is purpose-bound to a server-verified trial-link registration context, not office-admin selected-context validation; it cannot be used for other office writes. The public path uses verified link attribution inside `CurrentTenant::run()` with no fabricated `CurrentAccount`/admin membership; the actual Client model uses `App\Concerns\BelongsToAccount`, whose server-domain fallback supports this existing boundary. Prepared data stays process-local and expires with the request; it is not a trust-bearing client token. Public trial preparation must not expose an existing portfolio Client's details. Existing active same-office company Client reuse requires explicit `confirm_existing_association=true` after authenticated owner/CNPJ verification, and does not mutate its contacts, fiscal fields, certificates, monitoring or status. Archived/inactive Client matches return a domain conflict requiring office action; registration does not restore them silently. Unknown fiscal/tax fields must not be fabricated from the signup form.

Before commit, acquire locks in the established order: Account, office Subscription, office Plan, Company (or CNPJ advisory lock before insertion), contract, allocation, trial grant and counter/invoice as needed. Global identity email and CNPJ advisory locks use the existing access-transaction order before the commercial aggregate. Prepare required external CNPJ lookup before opening that aggregate transaction; then revalidate status, licence/module/client limits and trial-link revision under locks. Both same-CNPJ attempts and last-slot attempts must serialize to one winner and a domain 409 loser, not a raw uniqueness error.

Atomic registration includes identity/Client/Company/contract/subscription/allocation/trial grant. A Redis/session failure after commit is a recovery/login case: it does not delete the successful transaction or prompt another password POST. Use an actor/registration-bound idempotency key, store only nonsecret outcome IDs, and recover authenticated current outcome. Request password data and owner setup fragments never enter journal payloads, local/session storage, logs or durable evidence. CSRF, exact origin/private ingress and actor/IP throttles remain on public writes.

### 5. Define small, explicit API boundaries

All routes use `/api/v1` and existing response conventions; private replies use `Cache-Control: no-store`.

| Route | Auth/context | Contract |
|---|---|---|
| GET `/erp-commercial/enrollment-plans` | Office admin, selected Account | Allowed technical ERP plans, module snapshot and provisioning eligibility; no private cross-office offer catalog |
| POST `/erp-commercial/enrollments` | Office admin, selected Account, idempotency key | Exactly one of `client_id` / `new_client`; `erp_plan_id`; explicit `{price,currency,interval_months,due_in_days,start_on,billing_timezone}`; returns `data:{client_id,company_id,subscription_id,contract_id,invoice_id,owner_setup_status}` |
| POST `/erp-commercial/contracts/{contract}/owner-setup` | Same office admin; existing subscription maintenance | Name/email and explicit issue/reissue; purpose-scoped access continuation; no paid activation side effect |
| GET/PUT `/erp-commercial/trial-link` | Office admin, selected Account | Read/create/update selected technical plan and enabled state; writes use one command key |
| POST `/erp-commercial/trial-link/rotate` | Same guard | Rotate public code with original-operation recovery |
| GET `/erp/trials/{code}` | Public guarded ERP entry | Sponsor display name, plan/module summary, duration, availability and safe reason; no portfolio records |
| POST `/erp/trials/{code}/register` | CSRF/origin/rate guarded; anonymous new identity or authenticated owner | Verified company registration, explicit existing-association confirmation when required, command key; no caller-selected office/client |
| GET `/erp/trials/current` | Authenticated intended owner; no selected Company prerequisite | Current owned trial registration outcome for recovery; no unrelated identity/company lookup |
| POST `/erp-commercial/contracts/{contract}/convert-trial` | Office admin, selected Account, command key | Explicit paid plan/terms; same IDs and exactly one first invoice |

Use existing operation lookup for new office actions `customer.enroll`, `owner.setup.issue`, `trial.link.configure`, `trial.link.rotate`, `trial.convert`. Keep `invitation.expire` system-only. Client/contract detail and subscription readers add trial/owner-setup summaries. `/erp/trials/current` accepts a nonsecret registration operation key and resolves only the authenticated owner's matching committed outcome, with 404 for another actor; it does not choose an arbitrary recent Company. They do not expose raw passwords, owner fragments or seller private proofs. Validation uses 422 field errors, foreign resources 404, stale office context 409 `account_context_changed`, unknown outcomes remain recoverable, and business eligibility is an explicit structured reason rather than a generic fetch failure.

### 6. Decouple the core UI from retail-offer resale admission

Keep `can_sell_new` for legacy offer/invitation routes; add `can_provision`, `can_start_trial` and `can_manage_trial_link`. `can_configure` requires an active Account and real selected admin, not `erp_commercial.enabled`; existing policy maintenance remains available after office-subscription loss as D009 already allows. New paid/trial provisioning still requires an active office subscription. Licence limit and allowed module keys remain centrally configured; `0` is a real zero, and only null explicitly means unlimited. Direct/trial provisioning uses available quota and module intersection without requiring a published offer or the resale flag. Do not grant the office permission to edit its own platform quota. Explain zero capacity with a platform-plan action instead of inventing eligibility.

Keep authorization aligned on context, settings GET/preview/PATCH and operation replay. Office-subscription inactive or suspended Account rules remain explicit. Existing accepted-contract financial maintenance continues under D009 as before; anonymous link registrations never inherit that maintenance exception.

Primary navigation becomes Visão geral, Clientes ERP, Assinaturas, Faturas, Recebimentos, Configurações. Hide Offers only from primary tabs/sidebar/search; `/gestao-erp/planos`, APIs, frozen legacy contracts and already issued owner links remain valid. Remove only the duplicated `currentAccount.name` header slot; preserve the actual office selector and context assertion. `/gestao-erp/clientes` shows customer+plan+state+trial-end/next-billing with “Cadastrar cliente ERP”, “Selecionar da carteira” and “Copiar link de teste”. `/gestao-erp/clientes/novo` takes new client or portfolio selection and direct terms, with optional owner setup after success. Reuse customer components and `useOfficeErpCommand`; do not manually create another form shell.

`/erp/testar/[code]` uses the existing registration form patterns outside the office shell. It attempts identity refresh but does not require office membership or a previously selected Company. Its state machine is availability -> form -> submitting -> confirmed or recovery; definitive validation remains editable and ambiguous post-commit results become read-only recovery/login. Display "Teste de 7 dias", the sponsor and exact end date. Trial view uses "Em teste" and "Teste encerrado"; paid conversion never happens from a client-side timer.

Keep actionable business restriction panels distinct from subtle retryable fetch errors; a 403/409 authority change clears protected data, while same-context transient failures can preserve marked prior confirmed data. Apply Nuxt UI/Impeccable instructions and official installed component examples at implementation time; planning creates no new UI primitives.

### 7. Expiration and conversion must share one eligibility decision

`ErpTrialLifecycle::expire(ErpSubscriptionTrial $trial, CarbonImmutable $now): void` and `OfficeErpCustomerEnrollment::convertTrial(...)` lock the same Company/contract/subscription/allocation aggregate. Expire only a still-trial subscription whose grant has `ends_at <= now`; mark trial provenance and release once. `ErpCommercialGate::allowsOperatingWrites()` enforces the same cutoff synchronously, and queued producers recheck it before effects. Add a bounded hourly expiry command; its delay cannot extend access. Preserve `erp_trial_expired` for expired-trial denial and the existing `erp_subscription_inactive` for unrelated pending/canceled/suspended subscriptions; expose a domain denial reason from the commercial gate instead of guessing it in the UI.

Paid enrollment/conversion accepts `start_on` only at or before the office's current local date; a future paid start is not silently activated and returns field validation in this first slice. Trial timestamps use absolute elapsed UTC time; paid periods still use `OfficeErpCalendar` in office timezone. `OfficeErpRecurringBilling` explicitly ignores trial grants with no paid effective anchor/cursor. A manual/security hold remains after expiration or conversion; no auto-resume side effect is allowed.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- New unclaimed Companies change provisioning representation -> use explicit state, no placeholder users; prove operating access remains denied before and after claim attempts by foreign actors.
- Legacy modules assume only five subscription statuses -> update status enum, database checks, serializers, resource filters and UI status labels together; unknown values remain denied.
- An existing Client creation depends on external CNPJ data -> preserve lookup failure semantics and keep the provider outside aggregate locks; do not bypass the provider with fabricated fields.
- Concurrent registration may create email/CNPJ/capacity conflicts -> retain advisory/row locks, unique constraints and rollback-safe 409 translation; prove with independent PostgreSQL/Redis processes.
- Trial/signup endpoints run without tenant membership -> resolve office from the verified public-link row only; public transport allowlist must match exact route boundaries and strip caller tenant headers.
- A shared link can be passed onward -> rate limit, rotate/revoke, validate active sponsor/capacity on every commit and enforce one trial per normalized CNPJ; do not treat its possession as ownership proof.
- Active-only release guards may reject new trial values or accidentally allow fiscal sends -> centralize trial entitlement but retain all fiscal/provider/operating gates and prove no external dispatch.
- Expired trials remain readable and retain one grant forever -> this preserves customer data/anti-repeat attribution; trial history is deliberately not a deletion/restart interface.
- Main may advance after this planning snapshot -> merge the accepted main snapshot intentionally before implementation and revalidate these deltas; preserve its archived changes instead of recreating old active directories.

## Migration Plan

1. Implement and test forward-only schema additions on isolated PostgreSQL/Redis. Rehearse existing paid, pending, suspended and legacy invitation rows; default provenance must not alter their grants, cursors, ownership or invoice snapshots.
2. Add fields/tables and status constraint support, then deploy compatible backend reads before the new write paths and UI. Existing Company rows are claimed; only new administrative provisioning creates `unclaimed` rows.
3. Add a separate `ERP_OFFICE_TRIAL_ENABLED=false` gate in `backend/config/erp.php` and `.env.example` documentation; do not modify existing `.env` or flip `ERP_REGISTRATION_ENABLED`, operating/fiscal/release flags or production provider settings. Read trial availability from backend. Existing general registration stays pending.
4. Validate route guards, exact expiry, recovery, legacy flows and the retained-data paid conversion on a private guarded runtime; evaluate desktop/mobile light/dark and protected print routes.
5. Apply additions with `php artisan migrate --force --no-interaction`. Record evidence before accepting tasks. No `migrate:fresh`, DB reset, Redis flush, identity recreation or APP_KEY rotation is a deployment action.
6. Rollback is disabling new enrollments/trial issuance and restoring an app version compatible with the forward schema and trial statuses. Do not run destructive `down()` migrations or restore an app that ignores trial deadlines. Preserve trial grants, paid financial history, owner state and source-bound recovery data.
